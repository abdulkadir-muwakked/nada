Nada App Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 15, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Nada App ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use our mobile application ("App").

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • User Information: When you use our App, we may collect certain information that can be used to identify you, such as authentication information when you sign in.
  • Usage Information: We collect information about how you use our App, including your interactions with features and content.
  • Device Information: We collect information about the device you use to access our App, including device type, operating system, and unique device identifiers.
  • Biometric Information: If you choose to use Face ID for authentication, the App will use the biometric authentication features of your device. We do not store or process your biometric data directly; this is handled securely by your device's operating system.

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our App
  • Authenticate users and secure user accounts
  • Personalize user experience
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve the App
  • Display advertisements
  • Communicate with you about the App

Advertising and Analytics

Our App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use information about your device and App usage to provide personalized advertising. This information may include:

  • Device identifiers
  • IP address
  • Location information (if permission is granted)
  • App usage data

For more information about how Google uses information collected through AdMob, please visit Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

User Choices

You can control certain data collection and use through your device settings:

  • You can disable Face ID authentication and use alternative authentication methods.
  • You can reset your advertising identifier through your device settings.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device's privacy settings.

Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

Children's Privacy

Our App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top.

Contact Us
